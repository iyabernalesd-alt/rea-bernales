Shopify Store Build & E-commerce Execution

Building a complete daily care system in Shopify.

This shows my hands-on work building a Shopify store, setting up products, and getting them ready to sell. I handled everything from store setup to product pages to making sure orders run smoothly.

The Essential Duo bundle combines three products that work together for daily pet care. It includes customer reviews, educational content, and a clear offer to help pet owners understand the value and make a purchase.

The Essential Duo system

Three products working together to address the complete daily pet care routine.

Essential Duo combines FreshWipes for dental care, EyeWipes for eye care, and the Tear Stain Comb. Each product handles one part of daily pet care. The goal was to show them as a system, not separate products, so pet owners keep up with their routine. Each product needed clear messaging about what it does and why it works best together.

How it works in the store

Multiple sales touchpoints working together to move customers through awareness to purchase.

The Essential Duo was presented through product pages, customer reviews, comparison messaging, educational guides, and a money-back guarantee. The design made it clear what customers get and why buying the bundle is better than buying products separately.

Research and Product Strategy

Finding the right products to add.

Looked at customer feedback and identified gaps. What was missing from their routine? What would make sense to sell together? Selected products that solved real problems.

Three products made the cut. Tear Stain Comb, Dental Chew Bone, Dental Chew Ball. Each one at $15 and positioned where customers were ready to buy.
