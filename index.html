A little about me

I became the person who figures things out.

My background started in customer service, ecommerce support, and supplier operations. I learned that to understand how a business actually works, you need to sit in the middle—understanding customers, systems, processes, fulfillment, and the operational details that keep everything moving forward.

From there, my work moved deeper into ecommerce. But I brought that customer and operational mindset with me. I started working hands-on with Shopify technical, customer order management, CRO, fulfillment coordination, systems, and automation—always thinking about how these pieces affect both customers and operations.

Today, I work best in the space between strategy and execution. I don't need every problem to arrive as a perfectly written task. Give me the context, and I can investigate what is happening, figure out what needs to change, and help turn it into something that works.

I'm at my best when someone needs to understand the full picture—customer, Shopify, operations—and connect the dots to solve problems.
