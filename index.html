The Essential Duo system

Three products working together to address the complete daily pet care routine.

Essential Duo combines FreshWipes for dental care, EyeWipes for eye care, and the Tear Stain Comb. Each product handles one part of daily pet care. The goal was to show them as a system, not separate products, so pet owners keep up with their routine. Each product needed clear messaging about what it does and why it works best together.

How it works in the store

Multiple sales touchpoints working together to move customers through awareness to purchase.

The Essential Duo was presented through product pages, customer reviews, comparison messaging, educational guides, and a money-back guarantee. The design made it clear what customers get and why buying the bundle is better than buying products separately.
